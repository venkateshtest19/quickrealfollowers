1. Information We Collect

QuickRealFollowers is committed to protecting your privacy. When you place an order, we collect the information you voluntarily provide: name, contact number, and email address. We do not collect your Instagram password or any sensitive account credentials.

We use your information solely to:

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal information. All data transmissions are encrypted using SSL technology. We never store your Instagram password or payment information on our servers.
